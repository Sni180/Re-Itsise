Help & FAQs

Everything you need to know about reporting and tracking infrastructure issues.

🚀 The Resolution Process

1. Report

Submit your fault with a photo or voice note.

2. Verify

The municipality reviews and assigns a team.

3. Fix & Update

Track progress on the community activity until it is marked "Resolved".

How do I report a fault?

Click the "Log Report" button. To help technicians find the issue faster, try to include a photo that shows a nearby house number or landmark.

How can I track my report?

Once submitted, your report will appear on the Community Activity. You can locate your report to see the current status (Pending, In Progress, or Resolved).

Can I use the app in Setswana or isiZulu?

Yes! Use the "Select Language" dropdown in the sidebar to switch the entire portal to Setswana, isiZulu, Afrikaans, or isiXhosa.

What does the "I'm Affected" button do?

If you see an issue already reported by someone else, click "I'm Affected". This helps the municipality understand the scale of the problem and increases the priority.
