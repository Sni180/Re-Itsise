About Re Itsise

Re Itsise is a community-driven infrastructure portal designed specifically for the residents of the Northern Cape. Our name means "Inform Us" or "Let us know" in Setswana, reflecting our core mission to bridge the gap between citizens and local municipalities.

👁️

See

Identify infrastructure faults like burst pipes or power outages in your ward.

📢

Inform

Use voice reports or photos to log issues directly from your phone.

📍

Track

Monitor the progress of repairs in real-time through our interactive map.

Our Vision

By leveraging technology for social upliftment, we aim to empower rural and urban communities alike, ensuring that every resident has a voice in the maintenance of their environment.
