Help & FAQs

Find answers to common questions about using the portal.

How do I report a fault?

Click the "Log Report" button in the sidebar or bottom-right corner. You can type the description, upload a photo, or use the Voice Report button to speak your report in your preferred language.

Can I use the app in Setswana or isiZulu?

Yes! Use the "Translate / Change Language" dropdown in the sidebar to switch the entire portal to Setswana, isiZulu, Afrikaans, or isiXhosa.

What does the "I'm Affected" button do?

If you see an issue already reported by someone else (like a burst pipe in your street), click "I'm Affected". This increases the priority of the issue for the municipality.
